What it does

ClanBoard is a Telegram bot for one Clash of Clans clan. It follows the clan through the official game API and tells the clan group what happens, while it happens. Wars, the league, the Capital, Clan Games, who joins and who leaves: the group reads it without opening the game. The war board stays pinned at the top of the chat and keeps itself up to date until the war ends. Leaders get reminders, targets, leaderboards and the report of the week. It is all free, and nothing is unlocked by paying.

What arrives in the group

  • Wars and attacks: the board pinned on top, who still has to attack, the stars that are missing, the result when it ends — /war.
  • CWL: the week of the league day by day, the roster, who is in and who sits out — /cwl.
  • The Capital: the raid weekend, the districts that fall and the medals that come out of it — /capital.
  • Clan Games: the points of every member and how far the clan is from the maximum, announced while the event runs.
  • Movements: who joins, who leaves, who comes back, and what the clan looked like before.
  • Leaderboards and clan titles: the season of donations, stars, raids and games, and the titles the clan votes when the season closes — /points.
  • Family of clans: several clans of the same family under one roof, each with its own group.
  • Reminders and targets: who has attacks left before the war closes, and which base to hit — /targets.
  • Your own numbers, if you link your player account — /me.

In the group the bot keeps one pinned board, and speaks only for the urgent moments of a war.

Bring it to your clan

Write /newbot with the tag of your clan to the ClanBoard console on Telegram and confirm in the window Telegram opens by itself. The console builds a bot that belongs to your clan alone: one bot per clan, with its own name. Only leaders and co-leaders who have verified themselves can build one. Then take the new bot into the group of the clan and make it administrator, so it can pin the board of the clan.

Languages

ClanBoard speaks 20 languages, with English as the source and as the fallback. A clan picks its own with /lang; in private chats the bot follows the language of your Telegram. A better sentence is proposed with a pull request on the catalogs of the source code, with the contributor agreement signed inside the pull request itself.
